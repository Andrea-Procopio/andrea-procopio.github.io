BAINSA, the AI research lab run by students

Unofficial English translationOriginal (Italian)

From Bocconi to Stanford and MIT: the founders discuss BAINSA, Italy’s largest nonprofit artificial intelligence research lab run by university students.

BAINSA, an acronym for Board of AI & NeuroScience Associations, is Italy’s first and largest nonprofit artificial intelligence research lab run by university students.

The association conducts research in collaboration with academic institutions and industry partners, producing scientific papers supervised by some of the leading experts in artificial intelligence and published at the field’s top international conferences.

BAINSA also organizes events and lectures with professors and researchers, runs competitions and hackathons, and funds travel so that its members can attend international conferences and present their research findings.

Another key objective is to build a community of excellence that can develop future leaders in artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on research and entrepreneurship. The association aims to offer its members both a strong technical education and greater awareness of the ethical responsibilities involved in developing and using AI.

BAINSA selects the most capable and motivated students, united by an interest in artificial intelligence and a culture of altruism and giving back: a desire to share some of the opportunities and knowledge they have received with the BAINSA community and society more broadly.

Why was BAINSA created?

BAINSA was created to address a specific problem in the Italian academic system: the scarcity of opportunities for undergraduate students, including the most motivated and proactive, to take part in research from their first year of university.

Our vision is to build an environment, a community, and a social network that allow students eager to challenge themselves to access the same opportunities offered by Ivy League universities in the United States, or even better ones. In the United States, these pathways are already well established and integrated into the academic system.

Who are the students behind BAINSA?

“I’m Lorenzo Caputi. I study Economics and Computer Science at Bocconi University, and I will soon move to the United States to pursue a master’s degree in Statistics at Stanford University.”

“I’m Andrea Procopio. I also studied Economics and Computer Science at Bocconi, alongside Lorenzo, whom I met in class. I spent a period doing research at Harvard, which led to a thesis developed together with Lorenzo, and I am now starting a PhD at MIT focused on artificial intelligence.

BAINSA began within our university, together with other colleagues who helped establish it.”

Are there other associations that share your values?

Lead The Future is a merit-based Italian nonprofit association whose values of excellence, meritocracy, mentorship, and giving back we fully share.

What are BAINSA’s ambitions?

BAINSA’s long-term goal is to maximize the positive impact we can have on the world through artificial intelligence.

This means helping develop a future generation of leaders in AI who share a culture of giving back, passing on some of the knowledge and opportunities they have received, and who care deeply about ensuring that this technology is developed for the benefit of all humanity.

Research plays a central role in this vision. For an undergraduate student, it is one of the most effective ways to accelerate their career. Promoting access to research from the early years of university therefore helps develop future leaders who share certain values and a clear culture of responsibility.

BAINSA seeks to pass on this culture both to its members and to students who engage with the association through its selection process.

“We want to build a network of BAINSA branches at the world’s leading universities, each run directly by students. Together, they would form the world’s largest nonprofit artificial intelligence research lab run by students.

I expect BAINSA’s different branches and the mutual support among their members to give rise to many unicorns, companies capable of having a positive impact on the world, and researchers who will lead the future development of artificial intelligence.”

Who has BAINSA collaborated with?

BAINSA carries out projects in collaboration with some of the leading academic institutions and companies, including Harvard, ETH, Cambridge, and Bending Spoons, which provide direct mentorship to the association’s students.

“One concrete example of our projects comes from our robotics division: some students designed and built a robotic arm from scratch that can be controlled through brain activity recorded using electroencephalograms.”

Why focus specifically on research?

Because research is the most effective way to accelerate the career of a student who has just begun university studies in artificial intelligence.

Giving students the opportunity to enter the world of research from their early university years allows them to acquire skills and knowledge more quickly and gain experience that would otherwise be difficult to access.

At the same time, this approach helps develop a future generation of leaders in AI who have both in-depth technical expertise and a positive, responsible culture.

The ultimate goal is to ensure that those who lead the future development of artificial intelligence can maximize the positive impact of this technology on humanity.
