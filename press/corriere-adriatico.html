From Ancona to AI research at Harvard

English summaryOriginal (Italian)

Original article by Antonio Pio Guerra

The article follows Andrea Procopio from his final school year at Ancona’s Liceo Galilei to Bocconi University and research at Harvard. It describes his work on computer vision and the relationship between how AI systems and humans perceive the world.

It also covers earlier research at Marche Polytechnic University on lightweight machine-learning models for monitoring Parkinson’s patients through wearable devices.

Procopio discusses his ambition to build a technology company and the conditions that help young researchers develop their ideas. He reflects on differences in research infrastructure and intellectual openness between Italy and the United States, while leaving open the possibility of returning to Italy.
