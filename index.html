Abstract

My main interest is studying the principles underlying human visual intelligence as a mean for informed-design of human-like AGI systems.

Previously at the RIOT Lab (UNIVPM), I built tiny CNNs for wearables in the context of movement detection for Patients with Parkinson's Disease. Now at Harvard's CoCoDev Lab, I focus my efforts on investigating shared mechanisms of physical reasoning between computer vision models and humans.
