I’m a PhD student at the MIT Operations Research Center, working with Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas. Before MIT, I studied Economics, Management and Computer Science at Bocconi and did research at Harvard with Prof. Tomer Ullman. I also co-founded BAINSA, Italy’s first student-led AI research lab.

I work on AI systems that do research: they propose ideas, run experiments, and keep learning from the results. I’m especially interested in continual learning, in how these systems scale with test-time compute, and in how teams of agents can share research work.
